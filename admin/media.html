Add a clean video

Recommended for the TV display: no account name, no video title overlay and no player logo. The board uses the browser's own video player with the controls hidden, so only the picture is visible.

Easy setup
  1. Upload the MP4 to Dropbox or Google Drive.
  2. Encode as MP4, H.264 (AVC) video + AAC audio — older Android smartboards cannot play H.265/HEVC even when PCs and phones can.
  3. Click Share and copy the share link.
  4. Paste it below — the share link is converted to direct playback automatically.

A file already sitting in the project's videos/ folder works too: type videos/1.mp4.
